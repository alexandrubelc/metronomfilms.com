What's inside
Speed

Tab. Enter. Write.

Tab cycles elements. Enter creates the next logical element. Character names auto-complete. INT./EXT. pops up as you type. ⌘1-6 for direct element access. No mouse needed. Just flow.

FDX

Full Final Draft compatibility

Import any .fdx — 100+ pages, all scene headings preserved. Export back for production. Your script coordinator will never ask what software you used. Seamless round-trip.

Format

Industry standard. Always.

Courier 12pt. US Letter or A4. 1.5" binding margin. 54 lines per page. Auto-formatted as you type. Your page count matches Final Draft exactly.

Focus

The toolbar disappears

Auto-hide toolbar. Dark mode with near-black canvas. The page is all you see. Distraction-free isn't a mode — it's the default. Hover or tap to bring controls back.

Navigate

Scene navigator sidebar

Every scene heading, numbered, clickable. On a 100-page script, you're never lost. Click a scene, the document scrolls there instantly.

Smart

CONT'D and parentheticals

Same character after action? (CONT'D) added automatically. Type ( after a name — O.S., V.O. popup. Time of day learns your entries. The app adapts to your writing.

Reader Mode

You're a director reading before a meeting. A producer reviewing the latest draft. A writer doing a cold read of your own work. You don't want to edit — you want to read and think.

⌘⇧R. The keyboard locks. Right-click any selection — a dedicated annotation menu appears. No Cut, no Paste, no Spelling. Just highlights, notes, and export.

Yellow Green Blue Pink Purple

Select text, pick a color — instant highlight, like Apple Books. Add a note or just mark it and move on. Edit notes later. Clear one, or clear all. Everything saves in your .prs file. Come back next week, your annotations are exactly where you left them.

When you're done, export directly to Apple Notes. Every annotation grouped by scene, with the exact page number. Ready for the meeting.
